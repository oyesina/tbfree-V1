TB Free: Interview Field Sheet

Based on The Mom Test. Use one set of pages per interview. Record codes only, never names.

The 3 rules

  1. Talk about their life, not our idea. Do not mention the app first.
  2. Ask about specific past events (“last time…”, “walk me through…”), not opinions or the future.
  3. Talk less. Look for facts and commitments. Compliments count as zero.

Never ask: “Would you use…?” · “Do you think this is a good idea?” · “Would you pay…?”

Opening script (read aloud, in their language)

“I am learning how TB affects people here so health services can be better. I am not selling anything. It takes about 20 minutes. You can skip any question or stop at any time. I will not write your name, and what you say will not be linked to you. May I take short notes?”

A. Patient / recent patient / caregiver

  1. Walk me through the day you first noticed the cough. What did you do next? Who did you tell?
  2. Where did you go first? What did that cost, in money and days of work?
  3. How long from first symptom to starting TB treatment? What caused delays?
  4. Tell me about the last time you missed, or nearly missed, a dose. What was happening?
  5. Who knows you have TB? How did you decide whom to tell?
  6. Last time you looked for health information, how did you do it? Who did you trust?
  7. What have you tried to make taking the drugs easier? (workarounds)

Notes (write what they DID and SAID, not your guesses)

Scoring

☐ Specific recent example given   ☐ Already spends time/money/workaround   ☐ Commitment (trial, introduction, shared record)   ☐ Compliment only

B. Frontline health worker (DOTS nurse, CHEW, volunteer)

  1. Tell me about the last patient who stopped coming. What did you do?
  2. Show me how you record a case today. What is the most annoying part?
  3. Last time you referred someone, how did you know they arrived?
  4. How do you track a patient’s contacts, especially children?
  5. What do you already use on your phone for work? What did you stop using, and why?
  6. Last time something new was introduced at your facility, what happened?

Notes

Scoring

☐ Specific recent example   ☐ Existing workaround/spend   ☐ Commitment (2-week trial, shares register format, introduces colleague)   ☐ Compliment only

C. Policy maker / programme officer

  1. Last time a decision on TB funding or staffing was made, what data did you use? What was missing?
  2. Which report do you wait longest for? What did you do while waiting?
  3. Walk me through how facility data reaches you.
  4. Have you tried to adopt a digital tool before? What happened?
  5. Who must approve before a tool can be used in public facilities?

Notes

Scoring

☐ Specific recent example   ☐ Existing workaround/spend   ☐ Commitment (shares real data format, intro to programme, pilot sign-off path)   ☐ Compliment only

D. Community member / leader

  1. Last time someone near you had a long cough, what happened?
  2. What do people here say causes it? Where did that idea come from?
  3. Where do people here get health information? Who do they trust?
  4. If someone had TB, what would other people do?
